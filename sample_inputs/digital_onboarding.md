# Digital customer onboarding - sample stakeholder notes

The onboarding service must allow a customer to submit an identity document and complete a video verification session.

The service should verify the customer's identity quickly and securely.

The operations team must be able to review flagged applications and record the reason for each decision.

Customer identity documents should be retained for an appropriate period.

The platform must record who accessed an application and when the access occurred.

The service should be available during the bank's published customer onboarding hours.

Compliance must confirm which jurisdiction-specific KYC and recordkeeping obligations apply before production launch.
